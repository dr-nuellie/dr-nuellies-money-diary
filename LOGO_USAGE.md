# My Money Diary logo mapping

- `logo-app-icon.png` — selected square plum **My Money Diary** logo. Used **inside the app** for sidebar, top bar and unlock page, and on the printed monthly report.
- `app-logo-master.png` — identical square master for packaging.
- `favicon.png` and `icon-120.png` through `icon-1024.png` — square icon variants for browser tabs, iPhone/PWA icons and installation.
- `logo-wallet-mark.png` — old transparent symbol retained as a separate optional asset; it is **not** shown in the app header or unlock chooser.
- `logo-full-wordmark.png` — separate wordmark for optional editorial/print use.

No main app dimensions have been modified. Logo corners are rounded for use in the existing rectangular icon slots.
