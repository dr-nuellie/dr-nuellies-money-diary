# My Money Diary logo variants

- `logo-app-icon.png`: **square branded plum icon** with My Money Diary text. Use for installed app icon, favicon, PWA shortcut and About logo.
- `logo-wallet-mark.png`: **transparent wallet and green sprout only**. Use in the small header/sidebar brand slot and on the unlock chooser above the theme-rendered text.
- `logo-full-wordmark.png`: **transparent full horizontal/stacked My Money Diary logo** for optional print or broad branding, not for tiny favicons.
- `icon-120.png` through `icon-1024.png`, `favicon.png`, `app-logo-master.png`: sizes/compatibility copies of `logo-app-icon.png`.

The unlock title remains actual text, so its font follows the selected app theme. None of these logos changes the main app geometry.
